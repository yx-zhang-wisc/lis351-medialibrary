# MediaLibrary Planning Document

### 1. Purpose

MediaLibrary is a personal media review blog where I share my reviews on books, movies, and music. The website is designed to help readers discover new media and decide what may be worth reading, watching, or listening to based on my recommendations.

### 2. Audience

The primary audience is everybody who enjoys books, movies, and music. They can come here to see thoughtful recommendations.

### 4. Color Theme

The site will use a warm, minimalistic palette that fits a media blog and easily readable. It gives the website a polished, comfortable feel that matches the idea of a thoughtful review blog.

- Primary color: Warm beige (#F5F1EA)
- Secondary color: Slate gray (#64748B)
- Supporting color: Forest Green (#3F6B54)
